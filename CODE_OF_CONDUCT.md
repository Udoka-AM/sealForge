# Code of Conduct

sealForge adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any channel run for the project) and when someone represents the project in public.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and keep criticism to the work. Harassment, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, publishing someone's private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at **CONDUCT_CONTACT**. Every report is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct matters: report them as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on the impact of the behaviour. Maintainers who don't follow or enforce the code in good faith may face the same consequences.
